Sandėliukas Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

Sandėliukas is a pantry inventory app. It scans grocery barcodes and keeps a list of what you have at home, what you bought, ate or threw away. This policy explains what data the app handles.

What stays on your device

Everything you record in the app is stored only on your device, in the app's own browser storage: your pantry items, quantities, expiry dates, purchase history, product names you type in, and photos you take of products. The app has no user accounts and no server of its own. Nothing you record is sent to the developer or to anyone else.

Camera

The app uses the camera for two things: reading barcodes and, if you choose, taking a photo of a product. Camera frames are processed on the device. Barcode video is never recorded, stored or transmitted. Product photos you take are resized and stored on your device only.

Product lookups

When a barcode is scanned, the app sends that barcode number to the Open Food Facts family of databases (Open Food Facts, Open Beauty Facts, Open Products Facts, Open Pet Food Facts, run by the non-profit Open Food Facts association) to fetch the product's name, picture and nutrition facts. Only the barcode is sent; no personal data or device identifiers are included. Their privacy policy is at world.openfoodfacts.org/privacy.

Hosting

The app's files are served from Cloudflare Pages and GitHub Pages. Like any web host, they may log standard connection data (such as IP address and browser type) for security and operation. The developer does not use analytics, advertising or tracking of any kind.

Backups

You can export a copy of your data from the Settings screen. That file is created on your device and goes only where you put it.

Deleting your data

Use "Clear everything" in the app's Settings, or uninstall the app. Both remove all stored data from your device. Since no data is held elsewhere, there is nothing else to delete.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone.
